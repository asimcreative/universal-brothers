# How to Create a Hajj Package — Step by Step

For Universal Brothers office staff. No technical knowledge is needed. The same help is inside the admin: open **Admin Guide** in the menu, or press **Need help with this step?** on any step of the package builder.

---

## Before you start

- Log in at `/admin`. If this is your first visit, the dashboard offers a **guided tour** of two minutes. You can take it again at any time from the account menu (your initial, top right) → **Take the guided tour**.
- Have the brochure page for the package in front of you: title, code, days, hotels, prices in USD / SAR / PKR, the day-by-day plan and the notes.
- **The quickest start is a similar package.** In **Hajj Packages**, open the **…** menu beside a package and choose **Duplicate**. You get a draft copy with a new code, and the original is never changed. Or press **From a template**.
- You never have to finish in one sitting. Press **Save draft** whenever you stop. A draft is never shown on the website, and it reopens on the step where you saved it.

The builder has **14 steps**, listed on the left (on a phone, tap the **Step 1 of 14** bar to see them). A green tick means a step has what it needs. The **Checklist** under the steps, and the percentage above them, show how complete the package is. You can open the steps in any order.

---

## Step 1 — Basic information

1. **Package title**, as customers will see it. Example: *Executive Platinum Swissotel — Medinah First*.
2. **Package code**, your office code. Example: *UB025*. Each code can be used only once.
3. **Package tier / short title** and **Package series** (for example *Platinum — Non-Aziziya (Makkah & Medinah Series)*).
4. **Short description**: one or two sentences for package cards.
5. **Number of days**. **Length as shown** fills in for you (*14 Days Package*); change it if the brochure words it differently.
6. **Hajj year** (for example *2027*) and **Season as shown** (for example *1448 AH*).

The title, code and number of days are needed before the package can be published.

## Step 2 — Package setup

- **Arrival city:** **Arrive in Madinah** (Madinah first) or **Arrive in Jeddah** (Makkah first).
- **Hotel stay in Makkah:** **Non-shifting** (same hotel throughout) or **Shifting**.
- **Aziziya accommodation:** Included, Optional upgrade, Not included or Not applicable. If it is Included or Optional, an Aziziya card appears in step 5.

## Step 3 — Hotel options (A / B / C)

An option is a different hotel choice for the same journey, with its own prices.

- If every customer gets the same hotels, choose **No — one set of hotels**.
- If customers choose, pick **Yes — customers choose an option**. Option A and Option B are added. Give each a name customers recognise, usually its hotel (*Dar Al Tawhid Intercontinental*, *Fairmont Clock Tower*). Press **Add option** for Option C.
- The **What each option has so far** cards show each option's hotels, room types and "From" price, and what is still missing.

## Step 4 — Room prices

1. Inside the box for the right option, press **Add Quad, Triple & Double**, or **Add room type** for one at a time.
2. Type the **Price (USD)**, **Price (SAR)** and **Price (PKR)** exactly as the brochure prints them, in numbers only. **Leave a currency empty if the brochure does not give it — never guess.**
3. Switch **Available** off for a room type that is sold out, instead of deleting it.
4. Prices that are the same for every option go in the grey **Room prices for every option** box.
5. Check the **Price summary** at the bottom. It shows how prices will read on the website and the "From" price (the cheapest available USD price).

A Kaaba-view room or an extra night is **not** a room type. Add it in step 10, Additional options.

## Step 5 — Hotels & accommodation

1. In the right box, press **Add hotel**, choose the **City / place**, and pick the hotel from **Hotel from your list**. The name and stars fill in for you.
2. Or press **Find a hotel** to search your saved hotels, see each hotel's details and how many packages use it, and press **Add this hotel**.
3. Enter the **Nights**, and the **Meal plan** if you know it.
4. Missing hotel? Press **New hotel**. It is saved to your hotel list and selected.

**Saved hotel vs this package:** a saved hotel is shared by many packages. Anything you change in a row here changes **this package only**, and the badge on the row then says *Changed for this package only*. To change a hotel for every package, edit it under **Reusable Content → Hotels & Accommodation**.

## Step 6 — Journey plan

1. Save typing: choose a **Journey plan template** and press **Apply template**, or **Copy from another package**.
2. Otherwise press **Add day**. Each new day gets the next number and date.
3. For each day enter the **English date**, the **Islamic date** as written in the brochure (*08 Zil Hajj*), the **City / place**, **Where they stay**, **Transport** if they travel that day, and a short description.
4. **Fill dates** sets every date from the first one. **Renumber** numbers the days 1, 2, 3… again.

Example: *Day 8 · 14/05/2027 · 08 Zil Hajj · To Mina · Mina camp, Zone 1 · Private luxury bus.*

## Step 7 — Mina, Arafat & Muzdalifah

For each place, choose a saved arrangement from **Use a saved arrangement…** to fill the card, then change anything different for this package. A yellow note tells you the arrangement is shared and how many packages use it. Your changes here affect this package only. Leave a card empty if the package does not describe that place.

## Step 8 — Transport & meals

- **Add saved transport** and tick what the package includes, or **Add your own**.
- For a paid extra, switch **In price** off and enter the **Extra price**, **Currency** and **Price is for**. Example: *Jeddah Airport → Makkah hotel by car, USD 165 per person, round trip.*
- **Meals:** choose a plan under **Meal plan for every hotel in Makkah and Madinah** and press **Apply to all**. Then check the list; every hotel should show a meal plan.

## Step 9 — Included & not included

Two separate panels: **Included in this package** (green) and **Not included in this package** (red). In each, press **Add saved** to tick standard lines, or **Add your own**. A line already in the package cannot be added twice.

Examples. Included: *Ziyarat in Madinah with guidance*. Not included: *Airline ticket (approx. PKR 335,000 from Karachi)*.

## Step 10 — Additional options

Extra services customers may choose at extra cost. Press **Add saved option** or **Add your own**. Enter the **Option name**, **Price**, **Currency**, **Price is for** and any **Conditions**. Leave the price empty for "on request".

Example: *Kaaba View Supplement — USD 2,200 per person — subject to availability.*

## Step 11 — Notes & policies

There are four kinds of notes:

| Kind | How to add it | Shown on the website? |
|---|---|---|
| Customer note | **Write a note** or **Add saved note** | Yes |
| Important policy | A note with **Important** switched on | Yes, first and highlighted |
| Terms & conditions | A note whose **Kind of note** is *Terms & conditions* | Yes |
| Internal admin note | The **Internal admin notes — private** box at the bottom | **Never** |

**Internal admin notes are never shown to website visitors.** They are also never given to the AI assistant, never copied into templates and never copied to another package. Use them for reminders such as *"Confirm the Makkah hotel contract by March."* Never put staff-only information in a customer note.

## Step 12 — Photos & search engines

- **Main photo:** a wide photograph, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 4 MB. It appears at the top of the package page and on package cards. Without one, the website uses a stock photograph.
- **Social sharing image** (optional): used when the page is shared on WhatsApp or Facebook.
- **More photos and videos:** press **Add photo** and fill in **Describe the photo**.
- **Title on Google** and the description (optional): if left empty, the package title and short description are used. The preview shows how the result will look.

## Step 13 — Review everything

The whole package on one page.

- **Red — "Must fix before publishing"**: for example *Add at least one hotel* or *Option B has no room price yet*.
- **Amber — "Worth checking"**: advice that does not stop publishing. For example:
  - *The package is 14 days but the journey plan has 13 days.*
  - *1 room price has no SAR amount.*
  - *Option B has no hotel of its own yet.*
  - *There is no main photo.*
- Press **Edit** beside any line or section to jump straight to that step. The review updates as you type.
- The checklist item **Final review completed** ticks while you look at the review with no red problems, and is kept when you save. If you change the package afterwards, it un-ticks until you review again.

## Step 14 — Save, preview & publish

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save draft** | Keeps everything; nothing appears on the website |
| **Save & preview** | Saves, then opens the package exactly as a visitor sees it, with a yellow "Preview" bar. Check the prices in USD, SAR and PKR. The link works for one hour. |
| **Publish package** | Puts the package on the Hajj page straight away |

**Publish package** stays greyed out while any red problem remains, and **Fix these first** lists them, each linking to its step. After publishing, change anything and press **Save changes**; the website updates straight away. To take a package off the website, use **More → Move to draft**, or **Archive** it.

## Continuing an unfinished package

- **Dashboard → Unfinished drafts** lists recent drafts with how complete they are. Press **Continue**.
- In **Hajj Packages**, every package shows *N% complete*. Unfinished drafts have a **Continue** button that opens the step where you stopped.
- If you closed the page without saving, reopen the package on the same computer. The builder offers to **Restore** what you typed.

## Changing shared information safely

Hotels, transport, meal plans, services, additional options, Mina/Arafat arrangements and notes are saved under **Reusable Content** and shared by many packages. When you open a saved item that packages use, you see *"This is shared information. Used in N packages."* and three choices:

- **Update shared record**: changes the saved item for future use. Packages keep their current details until you open **Where it is used** and press **Update packages**.
- **Save as a new separate record**: keeps the original and its packages exactly as they are, and saves your changes as a new item.
- **Cancel.**

## Quick checklist before publishing

- [ ] Title, code and number of days
- [ ] Arrival, shifting and Aziziya chosen
- [ ] Every option named, with its own hotel and room prices
- [ ] Prices copied exactly from the brochure; unknown currencies left empty
- [ ] Journey plan complete, dates in order
- [ ] Mina, Arafat & Muzdalifah; transport and meals
- [ ] Included and not included lists
- [ ] Notes, with ticket & Qurbani marked Important; private reminders only in Internal admin notes
- [ ] Main photo and Google title/description
- [ ] Review shows no red problems; amber items checked
- [ ] Previewed in USD, SAR and PKR, then published
